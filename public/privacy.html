Privacy Policy

VIMAL College Hub provides academic-resource search. This page explains the information processed by the service.

Information we process

  • Email address and authentication identifiers to create and secure student accounts.
  • Search terms and daily search counts to provide the search service and enforce the free daily limit.
  • Payment identifiers and membership expiry information to verify ₹10 monthly passes. Card, UPI and bank credentials are handled by the payment provider; we do not store them.
  • Basic technical logs needed to protect the service and diagnose errors.

Advertising

If Google AdSense is enabled and the site is approved, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements, subject to applicable settings, notices and consent requirements. Sponsored placements should be clearly labelled.

Sharing

We use Supabase for authentication, database and file storage; Vercel for hosting; and Razorpay for payment processing. We do not sell student search histories. Do not enter passwords, sensitive personal details or confidential information into the search box.

Retention and requests

Account, payment and usage records are retained only as needed for account operation, fraud prevention, legal and accounting obligations. Contact the site administrator through the contact channel published on the website to request account or privacy assistance.

Children and student accounts

Use the service only if you are legally able to consent to the processing required for the service, or with appropriate parent/guardian involvement where required.

Updates

This policy should be reviewed and updated with the operator's legal name, contact details, retention periods and applicable notices before public launch.
